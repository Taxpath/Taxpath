⚠️ Common Mistakes

Most Taxpayers Lose Money Because They Ignore These

Avoid these costly errors that drain thousands from your pocket every year.

⚠️ AIS/TIS
01

Not Checking AIS / TIS

Annual Information Statement contains all your financial transactions. Ignoring it causes mismatches and notices.

⚠️ Form 26AS
02

Ignoring Form 26AS Mismatches

Differences in TDS credited vs 26AS leads to demand notices. Reconciliation is mandatory before filing.

⚠️ Deductions
03

Missing Deductions & Exemptions

80C, 80D, HRA, LTA, NPS, home loan interest — lakhs left unclaimed every year due to poor planning.

⚠️ TCS Refund
04

Not Claiming TCS Refunds

TCS on LRS, car purchases, and foreign remittances can be claimed back. Most people don't even know this.

⚠️ Wrong ITR
05

Filing Wrong ITR Form

ITR-1, 2, 3, 4 — filing the wrong form makes your return defective and can attract scrutiny or penalties.

⚠️ Deadline
06

Missing Filing Deadlines

Late filing means penalty up to ₹5,000, loss of carry-forward losses, and interest under 234A/234B/234C.
